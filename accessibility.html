Accessibility

Accessibilitystatement

Last reviewed: November 12, 2026.

Aerial Capture Photography wants everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and the WCAG 2.2 additions where practical.

What we have done

  • A “Skip to content” link, page landmarks, a logical heading order and a visible keyboard focus indicator on every page.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, including the menu, the gallery filters and the photo and video viewers (Escape closes them).
  • Photographs have descriptive text alternatives. The video clips have no sound, so they need no captions.
  • The background video can be paused with the “Pause background video” button, and video, smooth scrolling and animations stop when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Text meets WCAG AA colour contrast, and pages reflow without sideways scrolling down to 320 pixels wide and at 200 % zoom.
  • Form fields have visible labels and required fields are marked and announced.
  • Pages are checked with automated tools (axe-core) and by hand with the keyboard.

Feedback and help

If anything on the site is hard to use, or you need a photo described or information in another format, email aerialcapturephotography@gmail.com. Tell us the page and the problem and we will reply within 2 business days.
