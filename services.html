Questions

Before you book

Can you fly where my property is?

Most places, yes. Every location gets an airspace check first, and where the rules ask for authorization I request it before the shoot, so the flight is legal under Part 107.

Can I get photos and video from one shoot?

Yes. Stills and 4K video come from the same flight, so one booking covers the listing photos and the fly-through.

What if the weather is bad?

Every flight is planned around the light and the wind. If the forecast won't give a good frame, we pick another window together.

What do you need from me?

Three things: the place, a rough date and what the footage is for. That sets the moves, the format and the delivery.
