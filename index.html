How a shoot works

From first message to final files

  1. 01

    Plan

    Tell me the location, the subject and what the footage is for. I check the airspace, the weather and the light, and pick the window that gives the best frame.

  2. 02

    Fly

    On site I fly the shot list plus the moments nobody planned for. Stills and 4K video from the same flight, so you never choose between them.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Edited, color-graded photos and video sized for listings, web, social and print, delivered through a private download link.
