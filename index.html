Automotive

Cars look different from above

Hero shots, top-downs and collection coverage. From above, a warehouse of air-cooled Porsches turns into a pattern, and a single car reads like a graphic. I plan the light, the angle and the line so the car is the whole story.

  • Top-down and orbit stills
  • Collection and dealer coverage
  • Rolling and static video
02
Cities

Old cities at first light

Rome at dawn, before the streets wake up and the light finds the Colosseum. From altitude a city turns into geometry: rooftops, domes and the lines between them. The best hour is always the quiet one.

03
Alpine

Mountains, at eye level

A village tucked under a peak, ridgelines wrapped in weather, valleys opening up below. Altitude puts you level with the mountains instead of underneath them, and the scale finally makes sense.

04
Wild

Far from home

Remote rivers and coastlines, open country, and the wildlife that lives there. Patience, distance and the right light. The work that pushes furthest from home, and the frames I wait longest for.

Services

Aerial photography and videography for automotive, landscape and travel. From a single hero image to a full cinematic edit, the deliverable is built around what you need the footage to do.
