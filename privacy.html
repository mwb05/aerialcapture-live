Privacypolicy

Effective date: November 12, 2026. Last updated: November 12, 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Aerial Capture Photography (“we,” “us”) collects, uses and shares personal information through aerialcapturephotography.com (the “Site”), and the rights you have, including under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA) and, where it applies, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA).

Information we collect

  • Booking and contact requests. The booking and contact forms collect your name, email address, the type of shoot, location, preferred date and your message. Sending a form opens your own email program with the request filled in, so it reaches us as an ordinary email to aerialcapturephotography@gmail.com.
  • Emails and messages you send us directly, including through Instagram.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) automatically receives your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the visit in its server logs.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not ask for sensitive personal information.

How we use it

To reply to you, quote and schedule shoots, deliver your photos and video, keep records of our work, keep the Site secure and meet legal obligations. We keep booking emails for as long as needed for the job and our business records, generally no longer than three years after the last contact.

How we share it

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the business (our email provider, Google; our website host, GitHub), when the law requires it, or with your permission. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not disclose it to third parties for their own direct marketing (California Civil Code § 1798.83).

Third-party content. To display the Site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and jsDelivr. Those providers receive your IP address as part of delivering the file. Our Instagram link goes to Instagram, which has its own privacy policy.

Your privacy rights

You may ask to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it or to delete it, and you will not be treated differently for asking. California residents have these rights under the CCPA where it applies. Email aerialcapturephotography@gmail.com; we will respond within 45 days and may need to confirm your identity (usually by replying from the email address you used with us). You may use an authorized agent with your signed permission.

Cookies and similar technologies (cookie notice)

The Site does not set its own cookies or use analytics, advertising pixels or other trackers. The providers that deliver fonts and scripts may log technical information as described above but are not used to track you. If this ever changes, we will update this notice and ask for consent where required.

Do Not Track

Because the Site does not track visitors over time or across other websites, it works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, and no third party collects such information through the Site.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Photos of people and property

Drone images can show people, homes and vehicles. If you appear in an image on this Site and would like it removed, email aerialcapturephotography@gmail.com and we will take it down.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above shows the latest version.
